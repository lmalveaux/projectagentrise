# Agent Rise Market Launch Notes

## Market position

Agent Rise is suitable for a controlled paid pilot. Its strongest value is that it combines prospect management, scripts, carrier references, goals, compliance reminders, training, and the Fresh Pour learning environment in one workspace designed for independent Medicare agents.

Suggested positioning:

> Agent Rise helps independent Medicare agents organize leads, calls, follow-ups, goals, scripts, and field training without the complexity of a traditional CRM.

Before a broad launch, complete a pilot with 5–10 Medicare agents and verify account creation, sign-in, saving, password recovery, mobile field use, onboarding, support, data deletion, compliance materials, and the distinction between included and premium features.

## Privacy and security language for About, Terms, and Privacy pages

Suggested customer-facing statement:

> Your client data remains yours. Agent Rise does not sell customer information or share it for advertising. We use trusted service providers only to operate and secure the platform. Access is protected by account authentication, encrypted connections, and account-level data controls.

Suggested security statement:

> Data is encrypted in transit and protected at rest. Passwords are securely hashed and are never stored in readable form.

After two-factor authentication is implemented and tested:

> Optional two-factor authentication provides an additional layer of account protection.

The final Privacy Policy and Terms should identify Supabase, Netlify, and any enabled integrations such as RingCentral, calendars, or Call-E as service providers. They should also explain data export, retention, deletion, and what information an integration receives when an agent chooses to use it.

Avoid claiming that Agent Rise never shares data with third parties because infrastructure and optional integration providers process information to operate the service. Avoid claiming that a user's password encrypts their workspace; Supabase hashes passwords and the platform protects stored data separately.
