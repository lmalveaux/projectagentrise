Questions

What's included in the first month $19.99 offer? +
Your first month of AgentRisePro at $19.99 includes full app access plus the Medicare Advantage Launch Blueprint PDF — a $4.99 value — free. After your first month, your plan renews at the standard monthly rate. Cancel anytime before renewal.
Is there really no credit card for the free trial? +
Correct. The 7-day free trial requires no credit card and no payment information. You get full access to the app for 7 days with no obligation. If you want to continue, you choose a plan at the end of the trial.
What is the Medicare Advantage Launch Blueprint? +
The Launch Blueprint is a field guide written from 25 years of Medicare agent experience — covering PDP, Turning 65, Medicare Supplement, DVH, and Final Expense. It's the guide we wish existed when we started. Available as a standalone download for $4.99, or free with your first month.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes. You can change your plan at any time from the Settings panel inside the app. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
What is AgentRiseProA's agency downline feature? +
The agency tier includes a dashboard for managing up to 5 agents in your downline — tracking their activity, shared carrier resources, and team performance. IMO and FMO bulk pricing is available for larger teams. Contact us for details.
Is this compliant with CMS Medicare marketing rules? +
AgentRisePro is built with CMS compliance in mind — PTC tracking, SOA enforcement, TPMO disclaimers, and Do Not Contact protections are built into the workflow. However, agents are individually responsible for their own compliance. Always follow current CMS Medicare Marketing Guidelines.
